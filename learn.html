Planning guide

The 10-minute insurance plan

Most people are either over-insured on things that don't matter or under-insured on the things that do. Here's how to get it right.

Car: insure the full market value, and add Acts of Nature

A low sum insured cuts premium but slashes your payout if the car is stolen. Acts of Nature covers typhoon, flood and earthquake damage, which basic comprehensive cover excludes.

Home: insure rebuild cost, not market value

Land doesn't burn. Insure what it would cost to rebuild — and don't forget contents; most burst-pipe claims are about furniture and electronics.

Travel: buy when you book, not when you fly

Cancellation cover only protects you from the day you buy. A single hospital night abroad can cost more than your whole trip.

Safety checklist

5 checks that prevent 80% of home claims

Before a storm

Move cars to higher ground, clear balcony drains, unplug appliances and keep documents in a waterproof bag.

Electrical safety

Get wiring checked every 5 years. Don't daisy-chain extension boards — electrical fires are our #2 home claim.

Plumbing

Replace flexible hoses every 5 years and know where your main stop-valve is. Burst pipes are our #1 home claim.

Going away

Use timer lights, pause deliveries, and tell a neighbour. Homes unoccupied 30+ days need to be declared.

Rainy-season driving

Avoid flooded streets, and never restart a car stalled in water — it causes engine damage that isn't covered without Engine Protect.

If it happens

Stay safe, take photos, and report on WhatsApp within 48 hours. Don't start repairs before our surveyor says so.

Life stages

Big moments change what your family needs

Check my life cover

Getting married

Add your spouse as nominee and consider term cover of 10 to 15 times your income, so one income can't leave the other stranded.

Taking a housing loan

Whether it's Pag-IBIG or a bank loan, make sure your life cover can clear it, and that the home itself is insured for its rebuild cost.

Having a child

Increase cover to fund education, and add a Waiver of Premium rider so savings plans continue if you can't work.

Nearing retirement

As savings plans mature, reinvest the proceeds for guaranteed income instead of leaving them idle.

Health & longevity

After 40: the 4 checks that add years

Most serious conditions are cheaper and easier to treat when caught early. Our medical team recommends:

  • Blood pressure every year: the leading cause of heart attacks and strokes.
  • HbA1c (blood sugar) every year: 1 in 4 Filipinos over 45 is diabetic or pre-diabetic.
  • Lipid profile every 2 years: cholesterol rarely shows symptoms.
  • Cancer screening: mammogram or PSA as advised by your doctor.

Fraud awareness

We'll never ask for your OTP

Fake renewal calls and “policy bonus” scams are rising. Here's how to spot them.

  • We only call from ${BRAND.helpline} and never ask for OTPs, PINs or card numbers.
  • Renewal links always go to instellars.ph — check before you pay.
  • No one can “release a policy bonus” for a fee.
  • Report suspicious calls on WhatsApp: type FRAUD.

Suspect a fake claim on your policy?

If you get a claim notification you don't recognise, report it in your account. We'll block the claim and secure your policy.
